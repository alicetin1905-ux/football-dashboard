Methodology. The Rank by control switches which outcome is ranked: away-win likelihood is the average of the home team's home-loss rate and the away team's away-win rate over their recent matches; home-win likelihood is the average of the home team's home-win rate and the away team's away-loss rate. Either way, BTTS likelihood is the average of the home team's home-BTTS rate and the away team's away-BTTS rate, and Combined multiplies the win likelihood by it, treating them as independent — a simplification, not a true joint probability. Fixtures where either team has fewer than 5 recent matches on record are flagged "low sample". Alerts are always based on away win + BTTS, regardless of the selected ranking. The Home/Away form badges on each row are exactly the matches those rates come from — the home team's own home matches, oldest to newest, and the away team's own away matches.
